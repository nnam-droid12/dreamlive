# Fresh-eyes playtest (10 minutes, no coaching)

Meta's advice: "Ask someone new to try your build without coaching. Can they get
started, understand the interactions, and enjoy the experience?"

## Before they start (you)

1. Open <https://nnam-droid12.github.io/dreamlive/> on a laptop in Chrome, sound on.
2. Hand the laptop over and say only: "Try this. Say out loud what you think is happening."
3. Don't explain anything. If they are stuck for more than 30 seconds, note it, then give
   the smallest possible hint.

## Questions afterwards (write their exact words)

1. What was your favourite moment?
2. Was anything confusing, or did anything feel broken?
3. When did you feel most "inside" it?
4. Was there a moment you were bored or waiting?
5. Would you show this to a friend? Why or why not?

Send me the notes and the times; every "stuck" moment becomes a fix.
