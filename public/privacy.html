Dreamlive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Dreamlive is a mixed-reality experience for Meta Quest. This page explains what information it uses.

What we collect

Nothing. Dreamlive has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It does not send any personal information, images, audio or usage data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To remember your dreams between visits, Dreamlive saves a few small things in your headset browser's local storage: the dream moments on your Memory Shelf, the "Bigfoot & You" counts (for example how many high-fives), and your last few in-app photos. This data never leaves your headset. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data for Dreamlive in your browser or headset settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Hand tracking and passthrough

Dreamlive uses your headset's hand tracking and passthrough through the standard WebXR features of the browser. Hand positions are used only on your device, while you play, to drive the interactions. They are not recorded, stored or transmitted. Dreamlive does not access the headset cameras' images.

Children

Dreamlive does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Dreamlive page on the Meta Horizon Store.
